# Micro-creator brief (India, ₹2–5K each, 10–20 creators)

**Who:** travel / college / "friend group" creators with 10–80K followers, Reels-first, who post their own trips with friends. Not itinerary creators. The content is the group, not the place.

**The ask (one Reel + one Story, their trip, their words):**
1. Open a trip on Chiang Pai for a real upcoming plan with their own friends (not a staged one).
2. Drop the link in their group; film the friends reacting to the first calls ("Will X be late to the airport?").
3. Post the verdict card or the recap when it resolves, with the link in bio.

**What we give them:** nothing but the product and ₹2–5K on delivery. No script. The only required line: *"pies are play money, nobody wins anything but the argument."*

**What we want them to say in their own voice:** the trip that never happens; the friend who backs out; the friend who plans everything; who paid for what.

**Do not:** say "bet", "win money", "cash"; show any amount of rupees on a prediction; use the app with anyone under 18.

**Measure:** new trips opened in the 72 hours after each post (`pnpm stats`, compare to baseline), and card opens from non-members in the route logs.

**Budget cap:** ₹50,000 total for the first wave. Stop if the first five posts produce fewer than 10 trips combined.
